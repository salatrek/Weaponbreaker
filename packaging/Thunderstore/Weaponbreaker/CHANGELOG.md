# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial packaged release with enemy and player disarming, skill scaling and per-enemy configuration.
- Enemies fight with 15% damage while disarmed and can retrieve their own weapons after a configurable delay.
- Restore original combat parameters after retrieval.
- Successful shield blocks protect the player's held weapon from disarming by that hit.
- Default enemy weapon disarm chance is 30%; configuration is synchronized from the host.
